In short

Who is Mocked Monkey

Mocked Monkey is the independent project of Alessandro Falchi, a songwriter from Bologna. Born on the street, it brings the same spirit to every stage: songs that tell a story and instruments he builds himself from old consoles and toys. In 2025 he released the concept album Marasma, about escape and listening. He's working on a new single, with a video shot on a Game Boy Camera.
