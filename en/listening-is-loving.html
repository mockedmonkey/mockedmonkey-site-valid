#listeningisloving · The manifesto

In a world full of noise, choosing to listen is an act of love.

  • Whoever stops on the street doesn't pay for a ticket: they choose to listen.
  • We listen to others. And to ourselves, too.
  • Running away is not the answer. Listening to yourself is.
  • Listening is the only way to truly change.

"I truly believe that listening, to others and to yourself, is an act of love. It saved me."

Alessandro Falchi

Who's behind it

Alessandro Falchi

Mocked Monkey is the project of Alessandro Falchi, a songwriter born in Foligno who grew up, musically, between the streets and stages of Bologna. After years of collaborations, study and experimentation, in 2025 he released Marasma, a concept album about escape and listening.

On stage he's alone, with guitar, voice, kazoo and the instruments from the workshop below.

Something almost always breaks during a show: a string, a cable, a piece of the workshop. It's part of the act. The songs, on the other hand, are serious: they're about escape, noise, and how hard it is to listen to yourself.

Where it all started

The street and the stage

The project was born busking: under the porticoes of Bologna, in remote little villages and in every place in the world that has been home for a while. #listeningisloving was born from the sincere exchanges with the people met on the street.

From Bologna's venues to festivals, all the way to an open mic in Los Angeles where the crowd sang the choruses. The spirit is the same as on the street. Only the number of cables changes.

The workshop

Every object had another life

It isn't nostalgia. The sound I was looking for didn't exist in any shop, so I took objects made for something else and transformed them. Each one carries a flaw, and that flaw is exactly what makes it recognisable.
