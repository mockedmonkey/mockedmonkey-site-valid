The game · Free, in your browser

Immovable: the game

Immovable is a song from Marasma. It's also a Guitar Hero-style game: the notes fall, you hit them on the beat. The most fun way to listen to a song all the way through.

  1. Open the game: it runs in your browser, nothing to download.
  2. Hit the notes on time as they fall with the music.
  3. Make it to the end of the song and try to beat your score.

Concept album, 2025

Marasma

A record about absence, escape and listening: to others, and to yourself.

The protagonist runs from everyone and everything, listening to no one, not even the voices in his head, which grow louder as the story unfolds. The escape pushes him close to self-destruction. Only at the end does he understand that running was never the answer, and he chooses to listen to himself.

Written and pre-produced by Alessandro Falchi · Produced by Davide Fasulo · Lyrics reviewed by Robert Lincoln Hackett · Artwork by Alessandro Burelli · Released 3 June 2025
