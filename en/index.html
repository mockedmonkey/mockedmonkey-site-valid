When you're too poor to have a band, a Wii becomes the kick drum. A kazoo becomes a synth.

Mocked Monkey is Alessandro Falchi. He builds the instruments no shop sells, chasing a sound that actually moves you. He's alone on stage, but it sounds like a band.
